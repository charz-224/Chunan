DEMAND–INFORMATION GAP

What consumers value ↔ What product pages show

The same 10 attributes are compared across the consumer survey and product database.
Normalized gap score: Gapj = (Mean Importancej - 1) / 4 - Information Coveragej
Interpretation:

  • Gapj = 0 → Priority and information are broadly aligned
  • Gapj > 0 → High consumer priority and low information coverage
  • Gapj < 0 → Information coverage exceeds relative consumer priority
