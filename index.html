Bridging the Information Gap


From a Simple Question to a More Complicated Answer

The Question

During a family trip, I wondered why high-quality agricultural products of Chun'an (a county from my hometown, Hangzhou) remained difficult for many consumers in the main city of Hangzhou to discover and trust.

What I Did

I returned to Chun'an to interview local producers and sellers, collected 300 consumer survey responses, built a database of 30 local products, and developed an explainable matching prototype.

What I Learned

The prototype could reduce an information gap, but it could not solve problems involving trust, production standards, certification, and transportation. I learned that effective technology must begin with a deeper understanding of people and society.
