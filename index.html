Bridging the Information Gap


A Data-Driven Study Matching Chun'an Products with Hangzhou Consumers






Chun'an offers diverse local agricultural products, yet urban consumers may not receive the clear and trustworthy information they need. This project uses survey data, a structured product database, and an explainable matching prototype to explore that gap.
