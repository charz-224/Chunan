Research Design


From Observation to a Testable County-Level Question

MAIN RESEARCH QUESTION

How can consumer data and product information be used to identify—and potentially reduce—market-access gaps for rural products in Chun'an County?

THREE QUESTIONS TO INVESTIGATE

Barriers: What prevents Hangzhou consumers from purchasing unfamiliar agricultural products from Chun'an?


Alignment: Do the product attributes most valued by consumers align with the information currently available for Chun'an products?


Technology: Are consumers willing to use an explainable, data-driven system that matches their preferences with suitable Chun'an products?

TESTABLE HYPOTHESES

Market Barriers: Multiple barriers constrain consumers' access to unfamiliar Chun'an products.


Demand–Information Gap: The attributes most valued by consumers may not be fully reflected in the information currently available for Chun'an products.


Explanable Matching: Clear product information and explainable recommendations are expected to be associated with stronger purchase and system-use intentions.
