Research Story


From a Trip to a County-Level Research Question

PERSONAL CONNECTION — WHY CHUN'AN?

I grew up in Hangzhou. During a family trip to Qiandao Lake in Chun'an County, I became curious about how this mountainous part of Hangzhou connects with consumers in the urban center. I began to wonder why communities within the same municipality could have such different market opportunities. That question became the starting point of this research.

LOCAL CONTEXT — TWO SIDES OF HANGZHOU?

THE SOCIAL PROBLEM

Chun'an has many distinctive agricultural products, yet their access to urban consumers may still be constrained by small-scale production, uneven standardization, logistics challenges, limited market visibility, and incomplete information about urban consumer demand.

THE PARADOX

Distinctive rural products do not automatically translate into effective access to urban consumers.
